Personal Inventory / Skills

Techincal Skills (Hard Skills)

  • Programming languages – Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, SQL, and C# are most common.
  • Algorithms & data structures – Core logic behind efficient problem-solving.
  • Version control – Using Git/GitHub to manage and collaborate on code.
  • Debugging & testing – Finding and fixing errors; writing automated tests.
  • Databases – Designing and querying relational (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB) databases.
  • Cloud computing – Familiarity with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • APIs & integration – Building and using APIs to connect systems.

    • Personal & Professional Skills (Soft Skills)

  • Analytical thinking – Breaking complex problems into smaller parts.
  • Attention to detail – Small mistakes in code can cause major issues.
  • Persistence & patience – Debugging and problem-solving often take time.
  • Communication – Explaining technical ideas clearly to non-technical teammates.
  • Adaptability – Learning new frameworks or languages as technology evolves.
  • Teamwork & collaboration – Working effectively in agile teams or with cross-functional groups.
  • Time management – Balancing multiple tasks, deadlines, or long-term projects.
  • Creativity – Finding innovative solutions and thinking beyond the obvious.
